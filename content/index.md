## Reasons I like Elden Ring

- The game teaches a genuine "get good" mentality where patience, learning, and persistence are rewarded
- The lore is incredibly deep, meaningful, and filled with tragedy, philosophy, and hidden connections
- The Lands Between and the Realm of Shadow feel unforgettable, mysterious, and mesmerizing to explore
- The bosses feel legendary, both in gameplay and lore significance
- The soundtrack is absolutely god tier and elevates every major fight and moment

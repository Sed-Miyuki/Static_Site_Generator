# The Tragedy of the Twins: Malenia and Miquella

![Malenia and Miquella](/images/twins.png)

> "My brother will keep his promise. He possesses the wisdom, the allure, of a god."
>
> -- Malenia, Blade of Miquella

Few characters in Elden Ring are as tragic as Malenia and Miquella.

They were born as twins, but both were cursed from birth.

Malenia carried the Scarlet Rot within her body, a disease so powerful that it consumed everything around her. Despite being one of the strongest warriors in the Lands Between, she lived in constant suffering.

Miquella, meanwhile, was cursed with eternal childhood. No matter how much wisdom or power he gained, he could never grow into adulthood.

## The Unbreakable Bond Between the Twins

What makes their story so powerful is their loyalty to one another.

Miquella dedicated himself entirely to finding a cure for Malenia's rot. He abandoned the Golden Order and created the Haligtree in hopes of building a sanctuary for the unwanted and the cursed.

Malenia, in return, devoted herself completely to protecting Miquella.

Even after losing her pride, her body, and eventually her mind to the Scarlet Rot, her loyalty never wavered.

## The Battle Against Radahn

The tragedy of the twins reached its peak during the battle between Malenia and Radahn.

Unable to defeat Radahn through normal means, Malenia unleashed the Scarlet Aeonia, blooming into the Goddess of Rot for the first time.

The result was catastrophic.

Caelid was transformed into a wasteland filled with rot and death, while Radahn himself was driven insane.

Malenia technically survived the battle, but the cost was enormous.

## Miquella's Disappearance

While Malenia fought Radahn, Miquella remained inside the Haligtree, attempting to grow beyond his curse.

But before his plans could succeed, Mohg stole him away.

When Malenia finally returned, exhausted and broken from war, Miquella was gone.

That image alone summarizes the tragedy of their story:
a warrior who sacrificed everything for her brother returning home only to find she had already failed.

## Why Their Story Is So Memorable

What makes Malenia and Miquella fascinating is that neither of them are evil.

They are victims of curses, fate, and the cruelty of the world they were born into.

Despite possessing godlike power, neither of them could save the other.

That is what makes their story one of the saddest in Elden Ring.
